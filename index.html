FAQ

Useful answers before you book.

Only the questions that matter before a first visit.

What if I don’t know which service I need?

Choose General Consultation. The clinic can match you with the appropriate specialist after an initial assessment.

Can I choose my dentist?

Yes. Select a specific dentist during booking, or choose any available doctor.

Do your dentists speak English?

Yes. English-speaking appointments are available across the main treatment areas.

Are the prices on the website final?

Predictable services use fixed indicative prices. Complex treatment is confirmed after consultation and planning.

Do you work on Saturdays?

Yes. Both demo locations have Saturday appointment hours.
